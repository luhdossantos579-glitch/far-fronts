About The Far Fronts

The Far Fronts tells the WW2 operations and units the textbooks skipped, from official unit histories and veterans' oral accounts, on the map, without gore.

Who makes this

The Far Fronts is a military history channel on YouTube, published by Vision Media. Its research and scripts are original work. Narration is by a text-to-speech voice, as each video description states.

How we work

Every operation, figure and quotation is traced to a named source: the Army's official histories, the National Archives, oral-history collections. Those sources are given in each video description and at the end of every article. Maps are our own; photographs are public domain, openly licensed or our own, and the credits page records them per video.

Where to find us

The full series is on YouTube; all other links are on the links page.
